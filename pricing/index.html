Pricing

Pricing tiers follow the SG School Phone Pouch Programme infodeck. Request a formal quote to match your quantities and rollout plan.

Tier Best for Notes
School-wide Full deployment across all levels Follows published programme pricing. Request a quote to match student numbers.
Examination cohort Exam centre routines Uses the same pouch-and-base process; schedule aligns with exam dismissal.
Pilot Selected levels or classes Lets teams validate routines before scaling.

Final pricing is confirmed from the official tiers in the infodeck and will be customised to your quantities, timelines, and any approved options.

What affects pricing

  • Number of pouches required.
  • Deployment plan (pilot vs full-school).
  • Approved customisation options.

Included in every engagement

  • Programme guidance for students, staff, and parents.
  • Briefing on unlocking base routines.
  • Support for emergency unlock protocols.

Next steps

Share your estimated student numbers and preferred deployment window. We will respond with the matching tier from the infodeck and a formal quotation.
