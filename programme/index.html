Programme

A structured pouch-and-base solution that keeps phones sealed with the student for lessons, exams, and school events.

Programme goals

  • Maintain phone-free classrooms while keeping devices with students.
  • Provide visible compliance checks for teachers.
  • Ensure controlled unlocking at dismissal and during emergencies.

Core components

  • Lockable phone pouch (no needles; child-safe clip).
  • Unlocking base for end-of-day release.
  • Guidance for school staff, students, and parents.

Programme coverage

The programme supports full-school deployment, year-level pilots, and examination centre use. Phones remain sealed during curriculum time while staying with their owners.

Operations teams can designate where unlocking bases are placed and how emergency unlocks are authorised.

Support provided

  • Staff training on the locking and unlocking routine.
  • Student briefing materials for day-one readiness.
  • Guidance on emergency unlock protocols.

Programme highlights

  • Phone stays with student
  • Compliance visible throughout day
  • Unlocking base routine
  • Emergency unlock by admin staff

These elements align to create a predictable routine that respects student property and keeps classrooms phone-free.
