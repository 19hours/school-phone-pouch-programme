Programme overview

Programme

A lockable phone pouch plus unlocking base routine that keeps phones secured with students while giving schools visible compliance.

Book a demo How it works

Core components

Lockable phone pouch

Stays with the student all day. No needles are used; the child-safe clip secures the phone safely.

Unlocking base

Used at dismissal to restore access quickly. Staff supervise the process to keep routines orderly.

Emergency unlocking

Authorised admin staff can unlock immediately when needed.

Visible labels

Useful for students to see who the pouch belongs to, reducing mix-ups.

Where it fits

Morning arrival

Lock phones at the gate or foyer so every class starts phone-free.

During lessons

Teachers can visually confirm compliance without collecting devices centrally.

After school

Unlock at the base before dismissal, with clear lines to keep the flow moving.

Comparison vs phone lockers

Phone pouch programmePhone lockers
Pouch stays with student; checks are visible in class.Students travel to lockers for checks.
Unlocks at a single base under supervision.Requires key or code management across many doors.
