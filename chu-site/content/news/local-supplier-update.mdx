Chu Technologies Pte Ltd has invested in a new in-house sewing line to speed up production of lockable phone pouches for schools. The move eliminates overseas shipping delays and keeps spare stock available year-round.

The team also improved quality checks on tamper-evident seals, reducing replacement rates by 15% during pilot runs.

## What this means for schools

- **Entry workflow:** Faster replenishment means damaged pouches can be replaced the same week without disrupting form-teacher routines.
- **Storage vs carry-on:** Lighter materials keep pouches comfortable for students who need to carry them between venues.
- **Enforcement consistency:** Clearer numbering and brighter seals make spot checks quicker for discipline teams.
- **Admin effort:** Local support reduces back-and-forth with overseas vendors and simplifies procurement documentation.
