---
title: School Phone Pouch Implementation Guide
description: Step-by-step guide to launch a phone pouch programme that is safe and procurement-ready.
date: 2024-06-12
---

## Prepare your stakeholders

Brief teachers and admin staff on the rationale and workflow. Emphasise that the pouches are carried by students and remain locked during curriculum hours.

## Plan the collection workflow

Set up collection baskets at each classroom entrance. Students secure their devices into assigned pouches and place them in the basket during lessons.

## Daily checks and replacements

Appoint a student monitor to verify locks and replace any damaged pouches with spare units from the general office.

## Communicate with parents

Send a letter explaining the safety seals, unique numbering, and the school’s policy aligned to MOE guidance.
