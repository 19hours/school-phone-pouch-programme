## Why Hardware Plus Routine Matters

Phone distraction in classrooms is rarely solved by rules alone. The SG School Phone Pouch Programme combines **physical control** with **predictable routines**, allowing teachers to focus on teaching rather than policing devices.

The foundational rule is simple and visible:
**“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Use the Pouch to Eliminate Grey Areas

The phone pouch removes common enforcement issues:
- Phones cannot be discreetly used in pockets or bags.
- There is no ambiguity about whether a phone is secured.
- Teachers do not need to collect, store, or inspect devices.

Each student:
- Places their phone into a **personal phone pouch** on entry to the classroom.
- Secures it using a **child-safe locking clip** (no needles or sharp pins).
- Keeps the locked pouch with them throughout the lesson.

This enables **at-a-glance compliance checks**.

---

## Reinforce With Simple Classroom Routines

### Entry Routine
- Phone goes into pouch before or upon entering the classroom.
- Locking becomes part of the normal start-of-lesson routine.
- Teachers visually confirm that pouches are locked, without interrupting lesson flow.

### During Lessons
- The locked pouch remains with the student.
- No unlocking occurs inside the classroom.
- Phones are physically present but inaccessible, reducing temptation and anxiety.

---

## Support the Routine With Classroom Design

Schools can further reduce distraction by pairing the pouch workflow with:
- Seating plans that allow teachers to maintain line-of-sight
- Clear visual reminders (e.g. posters or slides) reinforcing the routine
- Consistent language used by all teachers when reminding students

Because the routine is predictable, reminders remain brief and non-confrontational.

---

## Keep Unlocking Low-Friction and Predictable

- Phones are unlocked only at **designated unlocking bases**, outside phone-free learning spaces.
- Unlocking occurs only at approved times (e.g. dismissal).
- Unlocking bases define **where and when** phone use is allowed, reducing negotiation.

Administrative staff can unlock pouches using an unlocking base in emergencies, without disrupting lessons.

---

## What Teachers Gain

When the programme is implemented consistently, teachers benefit from:
- Fewer interruptions related to phone use
- Reduced need for verbal reminders or confrontations
- Clear, visible compliance that does not rely on trust or checks
- More time and attention devoted to instruction and student engagement

---

## Key Takeaway

Reducing phone distraction works best when:
- The rule is simple and visible
- The routine is consistent across all classrooms
- Hardware supports, rather than complicates, enforcement

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme enables this by shifting phone management from **policing** to **routine**, allowing classrooms to remain focused and calm.
