## Purpose: Consistent Phone Management Beyond the Classroom

School events such as camps, excursions, performances, and concerts often involve movement across venues and external locations. The SG School Phone Pouch Programme is designed to extend naturally to these contexts using the **same routine**, avoiding special tools or parallel systems.

The guiding principle remains:
**“In phone-free activities, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Issuing Pouches for Events

### Student Pouches
- Students bring their **personal phone pouch** issued by the school.
- Where useful, schools may use **colour-coded pouches** to distinguish:
  - Groups
  - Buses
  - Levels or cohorts
- Colour coding supports faster visual checks by teachers and event supervisors, especially in large or mixed-group settings.

Colour selection is part of the programme’s optional customisation and is commonly used to simplify supervision during off-site activities.

---

### Visitors and External Participants

For events involving:
- External instructors
- Visiting students
- Performers or facilitators

Schools may:
- Hold a **small pool of spare pouches** for temporary issuance.
- Apply the same routine: phone into pouch → lock → unlock only when permitted.

This ensures consistent expectations without creating special rules for different participant groups.

---

## During the Event

When the event or activity is designated phone-free:
- Phones remain locked in pouches.
- The pouch:
  - Stays with the student, or
  - Is kept in the student’s bag, depending on activity logistics and safety considerations
- Supervising staff rely on **visible compliance** rather than device handling.

The programme works across:
- Camps and overnight activities
- Outdoor excursions
- Concerts and performances
- Inter-school or large-scale school events

---

## Unlocking at Event Conclusion

- Phones are unlocked **only at designated unlocking bases**, when phone use is permitted.
- Unlocking bases may be:
  - Temporarily placed at approved exit points
  - Managed by staff overseeing dismissal or dispersal
- Unlocking does not occur during the activity itself unless explicitly approved.

Using unlocking bases clearly defines the transition from phone-free time to permitted phone-use time.

---

## Administrative and Emergency Support

- Administrative staff or designated event leads should have access to an unlocking base.
- In emergencies, pouches can be unlocked by authorised staff in line with school procedures.
- Emergency handling should be briefed to all staff involved in the event before departure.

---

## Post-Event Review and Readiness

After major events, schools may:
- Check pouch condition and identify any replacements needed
- Return spare pouches to central buffer stock
- Review staff feedback on flow and supervision ease

These light-touch checks help maintain operational readiness for future events.

---

## Key Takeaway

The strength of the SG School Phone Pouch Programme lies in its **consistency across contexts**. By applying the same routine during school events, camps, and excursions, schools reduce confusion, minimise enforcement friction, and maintain focused, distraction-free experiences for students—on campus and beyond.
