## Purpose of Logo-Branded Pouches

School-logo pouches are an optional customisation within the SG School Phone Pouch Programme that reinforces school identity, improves ownership clarity, and supports faster visual compliance checks during the school day.

Branding complements the programme’s core design, where each student keeps a **personal, locked phone pouch** with them throughout the day, enabling visible and easy-to-audit compliance in phone-free spaces.

---

## Step 1: Confirm Branding Requirements and Quantities

Before production, schools should confirm:
- Total number of pouches required by cohort or level.
- Whether colour differentiation will be used across levels, houses, or year groups.
- Whether name card holders will be included for individual student identification.

Relevant customisation details:
- **Pouch colours:** Selection available (e.g. Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Purple, Black, Grey).
- **Colour customisation is free for orders above 1000 pouches.**
- **School logo printing:** Single-colour print on each pouch.
  - Cost: **$0.50 per pouch**
  - Minimum quantity: **1000 pouches**
- **Name card holder:** Optional add-on at **$0.50 per pouch**.

---

## Step 2: Finalise Logo Artwork and Colour Mapping

- Prepare a single-colour version of the school logo suitable for pouch printing.
- Confirm final colour allocation by level or cohort (e.g. different colours for different year groups).
- Ensure colour mapping aligns with existing school structures to avoid confusion.

Clear colour logic allows staff to:
- Quickly identify whether students are carrying the correct pouch.
- Perform fast visual checks during assemblies, CCAs, and examinations.

---

## Step 3: Coordinate Delivery and Issuance by Level

- Schedule pouch delivery in phases aligned with school levels or form classes.
- Issue pouches by cohort to reduce administrative load.
- Brief form teachers on:
  - Distribution procedures
  - Daily routines (lock-in, carry, unlock)
  - Replacement or top-up handling for damaged or missing pouches

Because pouches stay with students throughout the day, issuing them correctly from the start reduces future disputes.

---

## Step 4: Staff and Student Briefing Before First Use

Prior to first use:
- Brief staff on how branded pouches support visible compliance rather than enforcement through confiscation.
- Reinforce the standard policy message:
  **“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

Students should practise:
- Placing phones into their personal pouch
- Securing the locking clip
- Unlocking only at designated unlocking bases when allowed

---

## Step 5: Parent Communication

Include a clear explanation in parent communications covering:
- Why pouches carry the school logo (ownership, deterrence of loss, easy identification).
- How colour coding works across levels.
- Assurance that phones remain with students but are **not usable during lessons**.
- Confirmation that unlocking occurs only at approved locations using unlocking bases.

This clarity helps manage expectations and reduces questions about enforcement and exceptions.

---

## Step 6: Post-Issuance Review and Top-Ups

After rollout:
- Monitor usage during the first few weeks.
- Identify whether additional pouches or unlocking bases are required.
- Plan top-ups for future cohorts using the same branding and colour scheme to maintain consistency.

Schools may also arrange **on-site samples or demonstrations** to support leadership briefings or future expansions.
