## Purpose of the Parent Communications Pack

Clear and consistent parent communication is essential for the successful rollout of the **SG School Phone Pouch Programme**. Parents should understand that the programme is designed to **reduce distraction during lessons** while ensuring phones remain **with students and accessible in emergencies**.

The key message to reinforce across all channels is:
**“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Core Message for Newsletters and Circulars

Schools may adapt the following points for newsletters, email circulars, or school portals:

- Students will carry a **personal lockable phone pouch** during the school day.
- When entering phone-free spaces (e.g. classrooms, assembly halls, examination venues), students place their phones into the pouch and secure it.
- The pouch remains **locked during lessons** and stays with the student at all times.
- Phones can be accessed only at **school-designated locations** using an unlocking base, at approved times (e.g. dismissal).
- This approach avoids phone confiscation and provides **visible, easy-to-audit compliance** for teachers.

---

## Safety and Design Assurance

Parents often seek reassurance on safety and handling. Communications should highlight that:

- The pouch uses a **child-safe locking clip mechanism**.
- There are **no needles, sharp pins, or tools** involved.
- Unlocking is done using a **magnetic unlocking base**, operated only at approved locations.
- The design prioritises **student safety and ease of use**.

This differentiates the programme from other overseas solutions that rely on pin-based mechanisms.

---

## Emergency Access and Contact Assurance

Schools should clearly explain emergency procedures:

- Phones remain physically with students, even during lessons.
- In emergencies, **administrative staff can unlock pouches** using an unlocking base.
- Parents can continue to contact the school via existing channels (e.g. general office) as per current practice.

This reassures parents that the programme does not prevent urgent communication when needed.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (Suggested Topics)

Schools may prepare a short FAQ addressing:
- Why phones are not kept in bags or lockers
- When and where students can unlock their phones
- How lost or damaged pouches are handled
- How exceptions are managed where required
- What parents should do if they need to reach their child urgently

FAQs should consistently return to the same operational principle: **phones are present but not usable during lessons**.

---

## Format and Distribution

Recommended formats include:
- A one-page PDF summary for easy reference
- A longer FAQ hosted on the school website or parent portal
- Inclusion in termly newsletters or start-of-year briefings

Using the same wording across all formats helps avoid mixed messages and reduces follow-up queries.

---

## Key Takeaway for Parents

The programme supports responsible phone use by defining **clear boundaries**:
- Phones are secured during learning time
- Phones are accessible at the right time and place
- Student safety and emergency access are maintained

Clear parent communication strengthens trust and supports consistent enforcement across the school.
