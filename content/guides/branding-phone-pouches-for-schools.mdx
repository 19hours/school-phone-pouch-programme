## Purpose of Pouch Branding

Branding and customisation are optional enhancements within the **SG School Phone Pouch Programme** that help schools manage large student populations, differentiate cohorts, and improve day-to-day operational clarity for staff and students.

---

## Colour-Coded Pouches by Level or Cohort

Schools may select different pouch colours to visually distinguish student levels, cohorts, or houses. This is particularly useful in vertically structured schools or during assemblies and shared activities.

- Example usage:
  - Primary levels: P1 – Green, P2 – Yellow, etc.
  - Secondary levels or houses: distinct colours per level or house
- Available colours (up to 8 standard options):
  - Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Purple, Black, Grey
- Colour customisation is **free for orders above 1000 pouches**.

Colour coding allows teachers and administrators to:
- Instantly identify whether a student is carrying the correct pouch.
- Perform fast visual compliance checks without handling devices.
- Reduce confusion during assemblies, CCAs, camps, and examinations.

---

## Name Card Holders for Student Identification

Each pouch can be fitted with a **name card holder**, enabling clear identification of pouch ownership.

- Supports accountability by making it obvious which student the pouch belongs to.
- Reduces disputes over misplaced pouches.
- Useful during large-scale activities where pouches remain with students throughout the day.
- Optional add-on cost: **$0.50 per pouch**.

---

## School Logo Printing

Schools may personalise pouches with a **single-colour school logo print**.

- Reinforces school identity and ownership.
- Helps deter loss or unauthorised swapping of pouches.
- Creates a consistent, professional appearance across the student body.
- Optional add-on cost: **$0.50 per pouch**.
- Minimum order quantity for logo printing: **1000 pouches**.

---

## Operational Benefits of Branded Pouches

When combined with the core programme design—where each student keeps their locked pouch with them throughout the school day—branding provides additional operational benefits:

- Faster visual checks during lessons and assemblies.
- Clearer communication to parents that phones are present but secured.
- Easier enforcement of a simple policy message:  
  **“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

Branding complements, but does not replace, the programme’s core features such as child-safe locking clips, magnetic unlocking bases, and consistent routines across all school spaces.
