## Purpose: Consistency in High-Stakes and Shared Spaces

Examinations, assemblies, and CCAs are environments where **clarity, visibility, and consistency** are especially important. The SG School Phone Pouch Programme is designed to adapt to these contexts while maintaining the same core principle:

**“In phone-free spaces, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Examinations: Clear, Controlled, and Exam-Appropriate Handling

### Entry to Examination Venues
- Students place their phones into their **personal phone pouches** before entering the examination venue.
- The pouch is secured using the **child-safe locking clip mechanism**.
- Once locked, the **phone pouch is placed inside the student’s bag**, in line with common examination arrangements.

This approach ensures:
- Phones are secured and inaccessible.
- There is no phone handling during the examination.
- Examination conditions remain orderly and familiar to students.

### During the Examination
- The phone pouch remains:
  - Locked
  - Inside the student’s bag
- Phones are physically present but inaccessible, supporting both exam integrity and student reassurance.
- Invigilators do not need to collect, store, or manage student phones.

### After the Examination
- Students retrieve their bags and unlock their pouches **only at designated unlocking bases**, located outside the examination venue.
- Unlocking does not occur inside the hall or classroom, preserving a clear boundary between exam space and phone-use areas.

---

## Assemblies: Managing Large Student Flows

For assemblies, where students are seated without bags or with bags at their feet:

- Students secure phones in their pouches **before entering the assembly hall**.
- The locked pouch stays with the student for the duration of the assembly.
- Teachers and duty staff perform **quick visual compliance checks** (everyone has a locked pouch).
- Unlocking occurs only after dismissal, at approved unlocking base locations outside the hall.

No centralised collection or temporary storage is required.

---

## CCAs and Camps: Flexible but Consistent Application

For CCAs, camps, and other school activities:

- Students bring their **personal phone pouch** to the activity.
- When the activity is designated phone-free:
  - Phones remain locked in the pouch
  - The pouch stays with the student or in their bag, depending on the activity setup
- Unlocking occurs only at approved times and locations using unlocking bases.

This ensures a consistent routine across curricular and co-curricular settings.

---

## Administrative Support and Emergency Handling

Across examinations, assemblies, and CCAs:
- **Administrative staff** may unlock pouches using an unlocking base in emergency situations.
- Emergency procedures should be briefed in advance so staff respond calmly and consistently.

---

## Key Takeaway

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme adapts to different school contexts without changing its core logic:
- **Classrooms and assemblies:** locked pouch stays with the student  
- **Examinations:** locked pouch is kept in the student’s bag  
- **Unlocking:** only at approved locations using unlocking bases  

This flexibility allows schools to meet examination requirements while maintaining a single, schoolwide phone management routine.
