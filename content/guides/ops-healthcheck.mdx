description: A monthly audit template for discipline teams to track compliance, replenishment, and incident trends.

## Monthly audit steps

- Sample-check 10% of form classes for pouch condition, clip firmness, and visible numbering.
- Verify each class still holds a small buffer of spare pouches for transfers and visitors.
- Review incident logs: late unlocks, missing pouches, or tamper attempts.
- Refresh teachers on the mailto and WhatsApp escalation channels for urgent replacements.

## Data points to record

- Number of replacements issued and whether they were same-day.
- Which colours or levels show more loss to adjust colour coding or storage habits.
- Feedback from safety committees about the magnetic clip being child-safe and tool-free.

## Quick wins to implement

- Label shelves by colour level so students pick up the right pouch quickly.
- Rotate spare sets through ECAs to ensure all coaches use the standard routine.
- Add a short reminder slide to assembly decks about wearing the sling during transitions.

## Escalation workflow

If damage patterns appear, involve operations to check bags or lockers that may be pinching the clip. For repeated tampering, pair serial numbers with class lists and run a targeted briefing.
