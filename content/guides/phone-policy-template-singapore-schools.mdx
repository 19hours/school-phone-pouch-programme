Use this template to capture scope, storage method, exceptions, and enforcement steps. Pair it with the pouch workflow for consistency.

**Sections to include**
- Scope: during curriculum hours, CCAs, excursions.
- Storage: locked pouch issued daily; spare stock on-site.
- Exceptions: medical/ALP cases with teacher sign-off.
- Enforcement: spot checks, seal numbers, and escalation path.
