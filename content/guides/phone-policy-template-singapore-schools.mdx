## Purpose of This Policy Template

This template provides a **clear, enforceable phone policy** for Singapore schools using the **SG School Phone Pouch Programme**. It is designed to support visible compliance, reduce classroom distraction, and minimise enforcement friction for staff.

The policy should be read together with the school’s operational workflow for phone pouches.

Core policy message:
**“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## 1) Scope of the Policy

This policy applies during **phone-free periods and spaces**, as determined by the school, which may include:

- Curriculum hours
- Classrooms
- Assembly halls
- Examination venues
- Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs)
- Camps, excursions, and school events where phone-free time is required

The same routine applies across all settings to ensure consistency and avoid confusion.

---

## 2) Phone Storage and Handling Method

- Each student is issued a **personal lockable phone pouch**.
- When entering a phone-free space, students:
  - Place their phone into the pouch
  - Secure it using the **child-safe locking clip**
- The pouch:
  - **Stays with the student throughout the day**
  - Remains locked during phone-free periods
  - May be kept in the student’s bag where appropriate (e.g. during examinations)

Phones are **not collected**, stored centrally, or placed in lockers.

---

## 3) Unlocking and Phone Access

- Phones may be accessed **only**:
  - At **school-designated unlocking bases**
  - At **school-approved times** (e.g. dismissal)
- Unlocking does not occur inside phone-free learning spaces.
- Unlocking bases define **where and when** phone use is permitted.

In emergencies, **administrative staff** may unlock pouches using an unlocking base.

---

## 4) Exceptions and Special Arrangements

- Any exceptions must be:
  - Explicitly defined
  - Approved by the school
  - Communicated clearly to relevant staff
- Exceptions are handled in a controlled manner to preserve fairness and consistency.
- Staff should be able to recognise approved exceptions easily, without repeated negotiation during lessons.

Exceptions should remain limited so that the standard routine continues to apply to the vast majority of students.

---

## 5) Enforcement and Daily Compliance

Enforcement is based on **visible compliance**, not device checks:

- Teachers verify that students have their locked pouch in phone-free spaces.
- There is no routine checking of bags, pockets, or phones.
- Non-compliance is addressed through existing school discipline processes, not ad hoc phone handling.

The programme is designed to reduce disputes such as:
- “My phone is in my bag”
- “I left it somewhere else”

---

## 6) Stock Readiness and Replacements

- The school maintains a **buffer stock of spare pouches** to handle:
  - Damage
  - Loss
  - New intakes or transfers
- Replacement procedures are centrally managed (e.g. via the general office or designated admin point) to keep routines consistent.

Same-day replacement helps prevent operational exceptions.

---

## 7) Parent and Student Communication

The policy should be communicated clearly to parents and students, emphasising that:
- Phones remain with students, but are not usable during lessons.
- The system prioritises student safety and learning focus.
- Emergency access remains available through school channels.

All communications should reinforce the same simple message:
**“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Policy Outcome

When implemented with the SG School Phone Pouch Programme, this policy delivers:
- Reduced classroom distraction
- Fast, visible compliance checks
- Consistent enforcement across spaces and activities
- Lower administrative and staffing burden

This template may be adapted to reflect each school’s specific operational context while keeping the core routine unchanged.
