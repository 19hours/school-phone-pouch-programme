## What This Checklist Is For

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme is designed to deliver a **consistent, easy-to-audit** approach to reducing phone distraction in Singapore school learning spaces. The programme works best when routines remain predictable:

- Students place phones into a **personal phone pouch** when entering phone-free spaces.
- The pouch is secured using a **child-safe locking clip** (no needles or sharp pins).
- The pouch stays with the student throughout the day (and may be kept in the bag where appropriate, such as during examinations).
- Phones are unlocked only using a **magnetic unlocking base** at designated locations, at approved times.
- Administrative staff can unlock pouches during emergencies using an unlocking base.

Use this checklist monthly to confirm that daily execution matches the intended design.

---

## Monthly Audit Checklist

### A) Entry Routine (Lock-In)

- [ ] Phone-free spaces are clearly defined (e.g. classrooms, assembly halls, exam venues).
- [ ] Students consistently place phones into their pouches when entering phone-free spaces.
- [ ] Teachers can verify compliance quickly through **visible checks** (students have locked pouches).
- [ ] The routine is consistent across different spaces (classrooms, halls, CCAs, camps, exams).

---

### B) Unlocking Controls (Where and When Phones Can Be Used)

- [ ] Unlocking occurs only at **designated unlocking bases**.
- [ ] Unlocking base locations support smooth flow and clear boundaries (no unlocking inside phone-free spaces).
- [ ] Unlocking bases are functional and accessible at approved times (e.g. dismissal).
- [ ] Administrative staff understand emergency unlocking procedures.

Procurement reference for planning:
- **1 free unlocking base for every 100 pouches purchased**
- Additional unlocking base: **$80 each**

---

### C) Signage and Messaging Clarity

- [ ] Staff and students can repeat the same simple message:
  **“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**
- [ ] Phone-free spaces and unlocking locations are clearly communicated.
- [ ] Parent messaging remains consistent: phones are present with students but not used during lessons.

---

### D) Stock Readiness and Replacements

- [ ] The school maintains a buffer stock of spare pouches for:
  - damage
  - loss
  - new intakes or transfers
- [ ] Replacement processes are straightforward (e.g. via general office or designated admin point).
- [ ] Staff know how to direct students when replacements are needed, without creating ad hoc exceptions.

Pricing reference (per pouch):
- Tier 1: **2000 and above — $16**
- Tier 2: **1000 to 1900 — $17**
- Tier 3: **1 to 900 — $18**

---

### E) Incident Review and Operational Refinement

- [ ] Capture recurring friction points such as:
  - confusion about where to unlock
  - congestion at unlocking locations
  - damaged pouches requiring replacements
- [ ] Adjust unlocking base placement or supervision where needed to reduce bottlenecks.
- [ ] Refresh staff briefings if routines are drifting or inconsistently enforced.

---

## Success Criteria (What “Healthy Operations” Looks Like)

- Compliance is **visible at a glance**, without phone collection or device handling.
- Routines work across spaces, including assemblies, CCAs, camps, and exams.
- Unlocking remains controlled through designated unlocking bases.
- Parents and students receive a consistent message and predictable daily routine.

This checklist helps schools sustain a distraction-free learning environment with minimal additional administrative burden.
