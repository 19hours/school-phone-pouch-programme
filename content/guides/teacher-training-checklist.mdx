## Classroom briefing flow

1. Explain why the school is using lockable pouches and how it aligns with MOE guidance on digital balance.
2. Demonstrate the magnetic clip lock and show there is no needle or pin involved.
3. Issue the numbered pouch, name card, and sling to each student and note the serial in the class list.
4. Show the routine: device silent mode, insert, close clip, wear sling during transitions.
5. Share the unlock points for recess and end-of-day, and how ad-hoc unlocks work for learning use.

## Safety and inclusion reminders

- Visually confirm the clip is closed; do not force the mechanism.
- Offer colour-by-level pouches so relief teachers can quickly identify misplaced units.
- For students with accessibility needs, allow a bag attachment instead of sling carrying.

## Quick scripts for common questions

- *Can I unlock during class?* — Only when the teacher calls for it; the clip opens quickly without tools.
- *What if I lose it?* — Report immediately; we keep spare serials on-site for same-day replacement.
- *Is there data collection?* — No electronics inside; just a physical pouch with printed numbering.

## Handover to parents

Send a short note to parents highlighting the local Singapore supplier, the UEN 201926755K registration, and the child-safe magnetic lock.
