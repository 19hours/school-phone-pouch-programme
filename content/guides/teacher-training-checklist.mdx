## Classroom briefing flow (10–15 minutes)

1. **Set expectations and the “why”**
   - Our learning spaces are phone-free to reduce distraction and improve focus.
   - Bags and pockets still allow discreet phone use, so we are using a simple, visible routine.
   - The school’s key message is: **“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

2. **Explain what the programme is (in plain language)**
   - Each student uses a **personal phone pouch**.
   - When entering a phone-free space, students place their phone into the pouch and lock it.
   - The pouch **stays with the student throughout the day**, making compliance easy to see.

3. **Demonstrate the locking mechanism (safety emphasis)**
   - Show the **child-safe locking clip** and how it closes.
   - Explicitly reassure students: **there are no needles, sharp pins, or tools** used in this system.
   - Explain that unlocking is only possible with a **magnetic unlocking base**.

4. **Demonstrate the routine step-by-step**
   - Step A: Enter phone-free space → **phone into pouch**
   - Step B: **Close the clip to lock**
   - Step C: Keep the pouch with you; it remains locked during lessons
   - Step D: Unlock only at approved times/locations using an unlocking base

5. **Clarify unlocking rules (where and when)**
   - Unlocking happens only at **school-designated unlocking bases**.
   - Teachers should reinforce: students do not unlock inside phone-free learning spaces.
   - Explain emergency handling: **administrative staff can unlock pouches using an unlocking base during emergencies**.

6. **Show what “compliance” looks like**
   - Teachers will do **fast visual checks** (everyone has a locked pouch).
   - This reduces arguments such as “it’s in my bag” and avoids teachers handling devices.

---

## Safety and inclusion reminders (for staff)

- **Do not force** the locking clip; if a pouch appears faulty or damaged, route the student to the school’s designated replacement process.
- Emphasise safe handling: the pouch is designed with **soft material and rounded edges** and a clip that does **not** use needles or sharp pins.
- Maintain consistency across settings (classrooms, halls, CCAs, camps, and exams) so students do not receive mixed signals.

---

## Quick scripts for common student questions

- **“Can I unlock during lesson time?”**  
  No. The phone stays locked during lessons. Unlocking is only at approved locations/times using an unlocking base.

- **“What if I need my phone urgently?”**  
  If there is an emergency, the school will support. Administrative staff can unlock a pouch using an unlocking base when required.

- **“Does this work for all phones?”**  
  Yes. The pouches are **compatible with all phone models**.

- **“Why are we doing this instead of lockers?”**  
  Pouches make compliance visible and easy to check. Lockers make it harder to verify and can lead to disputes.

## Handover to parents (teacher-facing note / talking points)

Include the programme facts parents most care about:
- Phones remain with students but are **secured and not used during lessons**.
- The locking mechanism is **child-safe** and does **not** use needles or sharp pins.
- Unlocking is controlled through a **magnetic unlocking base** at approved locations.
