## What is the SG School Phone Pouch Programme?

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme is a schoolwide approach to managing student phone use in Singapore schools. When students enter **phone-free spaces** (e.g. classrooms, assembly halls, examination venues), they place their phones into a **personal lockable phone pouch**.

The pouch:
- Is secured using a **child-safe locking clip** (no needles or sharp pins)
- Stays with the student throughout the day
- Can be unlocked only at **school-designated unlocking bases** when phone use is allowed

The core policy message is simple:
**“In class, the phone is in a locked pouch.”**

---

## Is the locking mechanism safe for students?

Yes.

- The pouch uses a **child-safe clip mechanism**.
- There are **no needles, sharp pins, or tools** involved.
- Unlocking is done using a **magnetic unlocking base**, operated only at approved locations.
- The design prioritises student safety and ease of use, and avoids mechanisms commonly used in overseas solutions that rely on pin-based locks.

---

## Will my child still have their phone with them?

Yes.

- Phones are **not confiscated** and are not stored in lockers or collected centrally.
- The phone remains **with the student**, but is secured and not usable during lessons or other phone-free activities.
- This provides reassurance while still reducing distraction.

---

## When can students unlock and use their phones?

Students may unlock their phones:
- **Only at designated unlocking bases**
- **Only at school-approved times**, such as dismissal or other defined periods

Unlocking does not take place inside phone-free learning spaces.

---

## What happens during examinations?

During examinations:
- Students place their phones into their **personal phone pouches** before entering the venue.
- The pouch is locked and then **kept inside the student’s bag** for the duration of the examination.
- Phones are unlocked only after the paper, at designated unlocking bases outside the venue.

This aligns with common Singapore examination arrangements while maintaining phone security.

---

## How can parents reach the school or my child in an emergency?

- Parents should continue to contact the school through existing channels (e.g. the general office), as per current practice.
- In emergencies, **administrative staff can unlock a student’s pouch** using an unlocking base.
- The programme does not prevent urgent communication when required.

---

## What if a pouch is damaged or lost?

- Schools maintain a **buffer stock of spare pouches** for replacements.
- Replacement procedures are handled centrally (e.g. via the general office or designated admin point).
- Same-day replacement helps ensure routines remain consistent without ad hoc exceptions.

---

## Are there exceptions to the policy?

- Any exceptions are **explicitly defined and approved by the school**.
- Exceptions are handled in a controlled manner to preserve clarity and fairness.
- Staff are briefed so that approved cases are recognised without repeated negotiation.

---

## Why is this better than phones in bags or lockers?

- Phones in bags or pockets can still be used discreetly.
- Lockers make compliance harder to verify and can lead to disputes.
- The pouch provides **visible, easy-to-audit compliance** at a glance, reducing enforcement friction for teachers.

---

## How are parents kept informed?

Schools typically provide:
- A one-page PDF summary explaining the programme
- An FAQ document hosted on the school website or parent portal
- Termly or start-of-year communications reinforcing routines

Parents may also be provided with:
- A WhatsApp or general office contact for operational queries
- A formal quotation or procurement reference for transparency, where relevant

---

## Key Takeaway for Parents

The programme supports responsible phone use by clearly defining boundaries:
- Phones are secured during learning time
- Phones are accessible at the right time and place
- Student safety and emergency access are maintained

This allows schools to keep learning spaces focused while maintaining parent confidence and trust.
