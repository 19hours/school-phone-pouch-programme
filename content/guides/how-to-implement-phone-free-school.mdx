### Overview

Set a clear objective, communicate early with parents, and pilot with one level before scaling.

### Steps
1. Establish policy language and consequences.
2. Run a two-week pilot with form teachers and discipline leads.
3. Train unlock point staff and create signage for entry lines.
4. Collect feedback, adjust unlock locations, and roll out to all cohorts.

### What schools gain
Lower disruptions, simpler hall checks, and fewer confiscation disputes.
