Schools in Singapore follow MOE's stance that mobile devices should not disrupt learning. Most schools require phones to be kept away during curriculum hours, while still allowing parents to reach students through formal channels.

The School Phone Pouch Programme follows that principle by keeping devices powered and within reach for emergencies but physically inaccessible during teaching time. Local manufacturing means replacements and seals can be supplied within days so there is no operational gap.

## What this means for schools

### Entry workflow
Students secure their devices in the Chu pouch before the first period and hand them to the form teacher or place them in a class caddy.

Teachers use a short script: "Phones in pouches, seal checked, then take your seats." This makes classroom starts predictable for relief teachers and visitors.

### Storage vs carry-on locked pouch
The pouch can be stored in class lockers during lessons or clipped to bags for after-school programmes while remaining locked.

For camps and CCAs, the sling and name card help teachers confirm accountability even when moving between venues.

### Enforcement consistency
Unique numbering and tamper-evident seals make checks quick. Teachers only need to scan the class and verify that pouches are sealed.

Seals can be refreshed termly; damaged seals are a clear signal for replacement rather than a disciplinary debate.

### Admin effort
Minimal. Replacement pouches and spare locks are stocked locally by Chu Technologies Pte Ltd, so operations are not halted by supply gaps.
