## Suggested email copy

Subject: Implementation of Phone Pouch Routine to Support Focused Learning

Dear Parents/Guardians,

To reduce distraction during curriculum time while maintaining clear, consistent routines for students, our school will be implementing the **SG School Phone Pouch Programme**.

Under this programme, each student will be issued a **personal lockable phone pouch**. When students enter **phone-free spaces** (such as classrooms, assembly halls, and examination venues), they will place their phone into the pouch and secure it using the built-in locking clip.

The programme materials state that the pouch uses a **child-safe locking clip mechanism** and **does not use needles**. Phones can be accessed only when permitted, by unlocking the pouch at a **magnetic unlocking base** at school-approved locations.

The pouch stays with each student throughout the day, allowing teachers to confirm compliance quickly through visible checks, without collecting devices. In emergencies, authorised administrative staff can unlock pouches using an unlocking base.

If you need urgent contact during curriculum time, please call the **general office**. The school will facilitate communication as needed.

Thank you for your support in helping us create a calm, focused learning environment for all students.

Yours sincerely,  
[School Name]  
[Name / Designation]  
[General Office Contact Number]

## Key points to highlight (for school use)

- **Phones remain with students:** The pouch stays with the student, but phones are not usable in phone-free spaces.
- **Visible compliance:** Teachers can verify at a glance that phones are secured in locked pouches.
- **Controlled unlocking:** Unlocking takes place only at approved locations using unlocking bases, at approved times (e.g. dismissal).
- **Emergency readiness:** Administrative staff can unlock pouches during emergencies using an unlocking base.

## How families can help

- Remind your child to follow the routine consistently: phone into pouch when entering phone-free spaces, keep the pouch locked during lessons.
- Encourage your child to handle the pouch carefully and report any issues promptly.
- Reinforce that unlocking happens only at school-approved locations and times.

If a pouch is damaged or missing, the school will follow its replacement process to keep routines consistent.
