## Suggested email copy

Dear Parents/Guardians,

To reduce distraction while keeping students reachable after school, we are issuing lockable phone pouches. These are locally manufactured by Chu Technologies Pte Ltd (UEN 201926755K) and use a child-safe magnetic clip—no pins or needles.

## Key points to highlight

- Phones stay with the student in a pouch rather than in a distant locker, so dismissal is fast and orderly.
- Pouches are numbered and carry the school logo and name card for accountability.
- Colours by level make it easy for teachers to verify that all devices are secured.

## How families can help

- Remind your child to switch to silent mode before inserting their phone.
- Encourage them to wear the sling when moving between classes so the pouch is not left on desks.
- If the pouch is damaged, contact us for a same-day replacement. Local stock is available and serviced by a Singapore team.

## Disclaimer

This template summarises the school’s interpretation of MOE guidance around balanced device use. Always align with your school’s ICT and conduct policies.
