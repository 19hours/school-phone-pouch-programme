title: Common parent concerns and responses
description: How to address caregiver questions about the phone pouch programme.
date: '2024-08-15'
---

Reassure parents about emergency access, safety of the magnetic clip, and how phones are returned daily. Provide clear office contact numbers and escalation paths.

## Key responses to share
- Phones stay powered for emergency alerts but are inaccessible during lessons and exams.
- Magnetic clip uses no needles; unlocking bases stay with staff so students cannot self-release.
- Multiple unlock points reduce queues; staff can prioritise students who need to leave early.
- Colour-by-level and logo printing make it easy to spot any unsealed pouch.

## Suggested channels
- Add a one-page FAQ to student handbooks and the school website.
- Share a short video of the pouch being locked and unlocked to reassure families about safety.
- Provide a WhatsApp number for urgent questions during the first fortnight of rollout.

*Disclaimer: Align final messaging with your school’s discipline and emergency contact policies.*
