We summarise the latest MOE direction on reducing classroom phone use. Guidance highlights safety, well-being, and the need for predictable workflows.

**What this means for schools**
- Issue lockable pouches at entry to make compliance visible.
- Use colour coding and name cards for faster checks.
- Keep unlock points staffed to avoid bottlenecks after school.
