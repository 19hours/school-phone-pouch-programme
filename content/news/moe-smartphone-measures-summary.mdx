We summarise the latest MOE direction on reducing classroom phone use. Guidance highlights safety, well-being, and the need for predictable workflows. The language emphasises that phones can remain with students if they are inaccessible during lessons and examinations.

## Key takeaways
- Physical controls are recommended where digital controls alone are insufficient.
- Schools should provide clear routines so parents, students, and relief teachers know what to expect.
- Emergency access must remain possible without slowing evacuation or dismissal.

## What this means for schools
- Issue lockable pouches at entry to make compliance visible and auditable.
- Use colour coding and name cards for faster checks, especially for large cohorts leaving the hall.
- Keep unlock points staffed to avoid bottlenecks after school; mobile unlock kits help during events.
