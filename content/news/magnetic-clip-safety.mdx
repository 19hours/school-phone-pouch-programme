We completed a safety review of the magnetic clip used on the school phone pouch. The test confirmed that the clip closes securely without sharp pins or needles, unlike some overseas products.

## Findings at a glance

- **No puncture point** — the closure uses smooth magnets instead of a needle insert.
- **Child-safe release** — teachers can open the pouch quickly without a special unlocking base.
- **Durability** — after 1,000 open-close cycles, the clip retained strength and alignment.

## What this means for schools

- Lower risk of bag or pocket damage since nothing pierces fabric.
- Faster distribution because each pouch is self-contained; no need for a wall-mounted unlocker.
- Local supplier can swap out any worn clips with next-day turnaround.
