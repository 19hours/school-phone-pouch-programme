Schools combine policy language with hardware controls. Lockable pouches make enforcement consistent and reduce teacher workload. Many schools now specify “phones remain with the student but locked” to keep dismissal fast and avoid locker queues.

## Operational notes
- Morning issuance at form class with spare stock so forgetful students can borrow and return.
- Unlock points near exits; mobile unlock kits for events and wet-weather dismissals.
- Parent FAQs reduce friction during rollout; include safety language about magnetic clips with no needles.
- Form teachers can colour-code by level to keep hallways organised during cross-level assemblies.

*Disclaimer: Refer to individual school policies and MOE guidance for specifics.*
