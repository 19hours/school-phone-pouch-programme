Research links phone interruptions to reduced retention and slower task completion. Lockable pouches reduce temptation and deliver a consistent classroom baseline.

## What this means for schools
- Reinforce “phone in pouch” as the first classroom step so learning starts on time.
- Use numbered pouches to give students ownership and reduce resistance to checks.
- Pair the routine with cyber wellness lessons to build healthy device habits.
- Track disruptions and lateness before and after rollout to evidence impact.

## Suggested evidence to cite
- Meta-analyses on multitasking and learning outcomes.
- Local studies on mobile distraction during examinations.
