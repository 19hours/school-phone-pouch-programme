Countries are adopting hardware plus policy approaches. Singapore-made magnetic clip pouches avoid needle locks used overseas, keeping students safe while achieving the same outcome.

## Observations
- US and UK schools increasingly use lockable pouches; some still rely on needle-based locks.
- Australia focuses on secure storage at entry; mobile pouches make this routine flexible for camps.
- Singapore schools prefer solutions that keep phones with students but inaccessible for emergency readiness.

## What schools should emphasise
- Child-safe magnetic clip with no needles, suitable for primary and secondary cohorts.
- Local support, replacement stock, and vendor registration (UEN 201926755K) for procurement.
- Consistent scripts for relief teachers and vendors at events or CCAs.
