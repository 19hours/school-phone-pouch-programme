Pilot schools reported quicker morning settles and fewer confiscations. The child-safe magnetic clip avoided needle injuries seen in some overseas products.

## Lessons learned
- Start with a two-week trial for one level before scaling campus-wide.
- Pair the pouch routine with posters at classroom doors and a short announcement.
- Keep a small buffer of spare pouches in every block to handle damage immediately.
- Place unlock bases outside exam halls to keep aisles clear.

## Outcomes measured
- Reduction in phone-related reminders by form teachers after week two.
- Shorter queue times at dismissal when multiple unlock points are staffed.
- Higher parent confidence after receiving clear visuals of the clip and seal.
