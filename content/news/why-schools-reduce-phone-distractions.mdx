---
title: Why schools reduce phone distractions
description: Research-backed reasons to limit smartphones during curriculum time.
date: '2024-08-08'
source: 'Education research summaries'
---

Studies show lower focus loss and improved social interaction when devices are inaccessible. Hardware pouches give consistent enforcement without relying solely on rules.

## Key reasons cited
- Reduced multitasking improves note-taking quality and retention.
- Visible routines reduce conflict between teachers and students over ad-hoc confiscations.
- Parents appreciate that phones stay with the student for after-school coordination.

## Implementation tips
- Use a single script across all classes: “Phone in pouch, seal checked, then sit.”
- Place unlock bases where lines can form safely without blocking corridors.
- Refresh seals termly and log serial numbers for transparency.
