Problem

Schools need a phone-free environment during curriculum time without creating queues or storage risks. Administrators also need visible proof that every phone is sealed.

Solution

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme issues each student a lockable pouch paired with an unlocking base. Phones stay with the student, sealed and visible to teachers, and are released through a controlled routine.

Step 1 – Lock in morning

Students place phones into the pouch before lessons. The pouch locks without needles and uses a child-safe clip design.

Step 2 – Carry all day

Phones remain with the student throughout the day. The sealed pouch keeps ownership clear and supports visible compliance.

Step 3 – Unlock at dismissal

Staff use the unlocking base at dismissal to release pouches quickly and consistently.

Emergency unlocking

Authorised admin staff can unlock pouches when required without damaging the device or pouch.

Rolling out the programme

  1. Plan distribution and briefing with school leadership.
  2. Issue pouches to every student with clear user guidance.
  3. Train staff on unlocking base routines and emergency handling.
  4. Reinforce classroom checks for sealed pouches.
  5. Review feedback and adjust routines before examinations.

Comparison vs phone lockers

  • Phones stay with the student, avoiding collection and storage risk.
  • Sealed pouches make compliance visible in class.
  • Unlocking base supports an orderly end-of-day routine.
  • Emergency unlocks are handled by designated staff.

FAQs

How do teachers confirm compliance?

The sealed pouch is carried by the student, making ownership and lock status easy to see during class.

Who manages the unlocking bases?

School operations teams or appointed staff handle the base units for daily dismissal and for emergency unlocks when authorised.

Can the pouch damage devices?

The design avoids needles and uses a child-safe clip to protect both students and devices.
