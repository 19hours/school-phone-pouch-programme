SG School Phone Pouch Programme

Lockable phone pouches that stay with each student all day, keeping devices secured while lessons stay focused.

Designed for Singapore schools that need visible phone compliance without heavy infrastructure. The pouch unlocks at the base under staff supervision, and admins can override in emergencies.

Problem

Phones interrupt lessons, invite distractions, and make exam compliance harder. Schools need a visible way to keep devices secured from morning assembly to dismissal.

Solution

The lockable phone pouch keeps each phone sealed with the student, so bags and pockets remain compliant. The unlocking base restores access at dismissal, and authorised staff can unlock in emergencies.

How it works (Step 1–3)

Step 1: Issue pouches

Each student receives a pouch labelled to their class. The child-safe clip secures without needles, making it safe for daily use.

Step 2: Lock in the morning

Phones go into the pouch at arrival. Staff verify the pouch is locked for visible phone compliance before lessons begin.

Step 3: Unlock at base

At the end of the day, students unlock at the base station under supervision. Admin staff can perform emergency unlocking when required.

Rolling out (Steps 1–5)

1. Brief stakeholders

Align leadership, operations, and teachers on routines and responsibilities.

2. Prepare materials

Confirm pouch quantities, unlocking bases, and labels per class.

3. Train staff

Run short demonstrations on locking, checking, and emergency unlocks.

4. Launch with students

Issue pouches on a set start date and brief classes on daily check-ins.

5. Monitor & refine

Collect feedback weekly to keep the routine smooth.

Comparison vs phone lockers

Visible compliance

Pouch stays with the student, making checks quick without sending everyone to a locker room.

Faster routines

Morning locking and end-of-day release take minutes at the base, avoiding corridor congestion.

Emergency ready

Admin staff can unlock immediately when situations arise, without locating a specific locker.
