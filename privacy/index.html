Privacy

How we handle information submitted through this site.

Information collected

We collect details you submit via the Tally form, including your name, role, email, phone/WhatsApp number, estimated number of students or pouches required, preferred demo dates/times, and notes.

Purpose of use

Information is used to respond to enquiries about the SG School Phone Pouch Programme, provide pricing aligned to the published tiers, and schedule demos.

Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and support your rollout discussions.
