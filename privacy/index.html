Privacy

This site shares programme information for schools and collects enquiry details through a Tally.so form.

Data we collect

When you submit the Tally form, we receive the fields listed on the Contact page (school name, role, email, phone/WhatsApp, estimated number of students/pouches required, preferred demo dates/times, and any notes).

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss rollout timing, share pricing tiers, and schedule demos.

Retention and deletion

We keep your details only as long as needed to support your enquiry. You can request deletion or updates at any time by emailing kevin.chu@ct.com.sg.
