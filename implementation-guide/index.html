Implementation guide

A practical sequence to roll out the phone pouch programme smoothly and keep classrooms phone-free.

  1. Confirm scope and schedule. Decide which cohorts start first and set the date for pouch issuance.
  2. Brief leadership and staff. Align on enforcement, emergency unlocks, and unlocking base locations.
  3. Communicate with families. Share programme expectations and the rationale for visible compliance.
  4. Issue pouches. Provide every student with a pouch and simple instructions on locking.
  5. Run day-one checks. Teachers confirm sealed pouches during class transitions.
  6. Operate dismissal routine. Staff handle unlocking bases at gates or exits.
  7. Review and refine. Collect feedback from staff and students; adjust timings before exam periods.

Roles and responsibilities

  • School leaders: endorse policy and communicate expectations.
  • Operations teams: manage unlocking bases, replacements, and emergency unlocks.
  • Teachers: verify sealed pouches in class and remind students of routines.

Safeguards

Emergency unlocks are performed by authorised admin staff. The pouch uses a child-safe clip and contains no needles, protecting students and devices.

Implementation FAQs

How long does rollout take?

Schools typically complete issuing pouches and training within a short onboarding window, followed by routine checks in class.

Can we pilot before full deployment?

Yes. Start with a cohort or examination group, then scale once routines are proven.

How is compliance monitored?

Teachers perform quick visual checks; operations teams oversee unlocking bases and emergency access.
