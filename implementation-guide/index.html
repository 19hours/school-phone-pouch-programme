Plan and launch the SG School Phone Pouch Programme with a clear sequence: briefing, preparation, training, launch, and monitoring.

Rolling out (Steps 1–5)

  1. Brief stakeholders: Align leadership, operations, HODs, and exam teams on goals and routines.
  2. Prepare materials: Confirm pouch counts, unlocking bases, labels, and storage of spares.
  3. Train staff: Demonstrate locking, compliance checks, and emergency unlocks.
  4. Launch with students: Issue pouches, run class briefings, and set dismissal stations.
  5. Monitor & refine: Gather weekly feedback and adjust station placement to keep queues moving.

Communications toolkit

Parents and guardians

Explain that the pouch stays with the student and is unlocked at dismissal, keeping phones safe and lessons focused.

Teachers

Provide a quick checklist for morning lock-in, visual compliance during lessons, and end-of-day release.

Students

Share the routine and remind them that emergency unlocking is available via admin staff.

Operations guidance

Base placement

Position unlocking bases near dismissal points for quick release.

Queue management

Use clear lanes so classes release in minutes.

Emergency handling

Ensure administrators with unlocking authority are reachable during school hours.
