How it works

The SG School Phone Pouch Programme keeps phones with students while ensuring they are sealed throughout the school day.

Lock

Each student places their phone into the pouch at the start of the day. The locking mechanism secures without needles and uses a child-safe clip.

Carry

Students keep the sealed pouch with them, so teachers can see ownership and compliance at a glance in every lesson.

Unlock

At dismissal, staff use the unlocking base to open pouches quickly and consistently. The routine keeps queues moving smoothly.

Emergency unlock

Authorised admin staff can release a pouch immediately when a situation requires access to the device.

Classroom visibility

The pouch remains visible on desks or in bags, making it easy for teachers to verify that phones are sealed without collecting devices.

  • Device remains with the student all day.
  • Lock status is obvious without handling the phone.
  • Supports discipline while respecting personal property.

End-of-day routine

Unlocking bases sit at exit points. Staff tap the pouch onto the base to release it, reinforcing a predictable, safe dismissal flow.

Where exams are running, the same unlocking routine applies after papers end.

FAQs

Is training required?

We brief staff on the locking and unlocking routine and provide student-facing guidance so the process is clear from day one.

What if a pouch is damaged?

The pouch is designed to withstand daily use. If a pouch needs replacing, it can be swapped while maintaining the same unlocking process.

Can students personalise their pouch?

Schools can add single-colour logos, making it easy to see which pouch belongs to which school.
