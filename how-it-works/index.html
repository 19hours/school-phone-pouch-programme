Clear, repeatable routines

How it works

The lockable pouch stays with every student all day. Unlocking happens only at the designated base, supervised by staff, with emergency unlocks available for administrators.

Book a demo View the programme

Daily flow (Step 1–3)

1. Arrival check-in

Students place phones in the pouch at arrival. Staff visually check that the clip is secured without needles for a child-safe seal.

2. Lessons stay phone-free

The pouch stays with the student through classes, assemblies, and breaks, giving teachers easy, visible compliance.

3. End-of-day release

Students unlock at the base station before dismissal. The unlocking base handles the routine swiftly, and admins can override in emergencies.

Roles and responsibilities

Operations

Set up bases at entry/exit points and keep spare pouches for transfers.

Teachers

Check locked pouches during form class and monitor compliance through the day.

Admin staff

Hold emergency unlocking authority for urgent situations.

Supporting exams

Use the same routine for prelims and national examinations. Locking on arrival keeps corridors clear, and unlocking at dismissal maintains exam integrity while restoring access promptly.

What makes it safe

Child-safe clip

No needles are used. The locking clip is designed for daily student use.

Visible labels

Useful for students to see who the pouch belongs to, so bags do not get mixed up.

Emergency unlocking

Authorised administrators can unlock immediately when a situation arises.
